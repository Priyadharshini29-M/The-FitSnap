    Questions, answered

    What is virtual try-on for Shopify? add

    Virtual try-on lets shoppers see a product on themselves. They upload or take a photo on your product page and Brix-Tryon shows them wearing it, so they can decide with more confidence.

    How does Brix-Tryon work? add

    Shoppers click Try On This Look on your product page, accept the privacy notice and upload or take a photo. Brix-Tryon generates a photo of them wearing the product. They enter an email and phone number to unlock the full-size result, then add to cart, buy now, save the image or share it on WhatsApp.

    What does the free plan include? add

    10 monthly AI try-ons, a responsive widget for mobile and desktop, a privacy consent screen, try-on on the product page, standard AI processing and email support.

    How much does Brix-Tryon cost? add

    Free is $0 with 10 try-ons a month. Growth is $19 a month with 100 try-ons, and Pro is $49 a month with 500 try-ons.

    What is the Brix-Tryon AI Studio? add

    AI Studio is the merchant side of the app. It turns plain product photos into on-model images and marketing creatives, and saves every result in your Asset Library.

    What happens if I go over my try-on limit? add

    Extra try-ons are billed per use at the rate shown on your plan: $0.28 on Free, $0.15 on Growth and $0.08 on Pro. Try-on keeps working for your shoppers.

    Which products does Brix-Tryon support? add

    Brix-Tryon is built for fashion and apparel, and for accessories such as watches, jewellery and bags through AI Studio. You can enable try-on by collection or by product.

    Can I turn flat-lay or ghost-mannequin photos into model photos? add

    Yes. The Flat Lay to Model and Ghost Mannequin to Model workflows in AI Studio convert those photos into on-model photography, with no photoshoot needed.

    Does virtual try-on reduce returns? add

    It can help, because shoppers have less to guess about how a piece looks on them. Your Analytics page shows try-ons, cart rate and purchase rate, so you can see the effect on your own store.

    Can I customise how the try-on button looks? add

    Yes. In Widget Settings you can set button text, colours, radius, size, padding and fonts separately for desktop and mobile, with a live preview.
