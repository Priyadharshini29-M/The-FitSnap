FitSnap uses AI-based image processing technology to generate virtual try-on results.

Important Privacy Commitment

  • shield Customer photos are processed temporarily for generating try-on results.
  • shield Photos are not permanently stored after processing is completed.
  • shield Temporary image files may be cached briefly for processing reliability and are automatically deleted.
  • shield We do not sell, share, or use uploaded photos for advertising purposes.
  • shield Photos are never used to train AI models without explicit permission.
